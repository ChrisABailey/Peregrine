# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 September 2026_

Peregrine and its derived applications — including **Pippin** and **PythonView** — run entirely on your own device.

## What we collect

Nothing. These applications do not collect, transmit, or store personal data on any server. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no account, and no tracking.

## Where your data lives

All maps, routes, waypoints, tracks, settings, and any other data you create or import stay in local storage on your machine. The applications do not send this data off your device.

## Network access

The applications may fetch map, terrain, or chart data from third-party sources only when you explicitly request it. Such requests contain only what is needed to retrieve the data; no personal information or user content is included.

## Sharing is your choice

If you choose to share data — by email, AirDrop, the **Share** button, or any other means — you decide what is shared and with whom. Once data leaves your device at your direction, it is governed by the terms of whatever service or recipient you sent it to, not by this policy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the project maintainer.
